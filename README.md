# Air Quality Index Population Data Wrangling
Udacity Data Analyst Nanodegree Project 2

> As the Data Analyst in my cohort, I handled the aggregation and cleaning of public population data and EPA’s air quality data, focusing on exploring any correlations between the two. Identifying the worst offenders in terms of air pollution directly impacts their new Air Purifier product launch.

## Business Case:

- **Clean & Aggregate Data for Use:** Utilized Python to analyze IQAir index data and World population data. Performed data cleaning and EDA to explore and analyze the targeted 2017 – 2021 time series data.
- **Identify the top 10 markets for Product Launch:** Air Purifiers need dirty air to clean – knowing the worst countries and cities in terms of Air Polution would hierarchically rank the potential sales markets for the impending Air Purifier product launch.

![Screenshot of Population Impact on Air Quality Scatter Plot](https://github.com/sarahbeze/Air-Quality-Index-Population-Data-Wrangling/blob/main/air-quality3-1024x742.webp)

## Insights & Impact:

- **Population Directly Affects Air Quality Levels:** Overall, countries with larger populations tend to exhibit higher Air Quality Index values (i.e., poorer air quality).
- **CAir Quality is Worsening Over Time:** We also see that even if population remains similar, air quality has worsened from 2018 through 2021.
- **Top 10% have Rapid Degradation:** BThe outlier, a handful of high-population countries, the top 10% by population, show especially large jumps in their AQ index, indicating rapid degradation.

Together, the data suggests that while population size places increasing pressure on air pollution, policy interventions or technological improvements can moderate the effects, or if absent of any intervention, exacerbate those trends over time.

**Taking it further:** I would analyze the financial demographics of each of the locations as well as the current market size for air purifier sales, to identify potential areas that are likely to have the disposable income, the need for the air purifier, and that has an already established air purifier sales market. That could identify some less obvious but more cost effective markets to launch a product with less overall competition.
